# Run an exam

For examiners and organizers. This guide follows one exam from participant preparation to a reviewed result. If the server is not running yet, complete [Launch: dev & production](/start) first.

**On this page:** [Prepare](#1-prepare-participants) · [Configure](#2-configure-the-exam) · [Student admission](#3-prepare-the-student-and-start) · [Monitor](#4-monitor-the-attempt) · [Review](#5-review-and-record-the-outcome)

## 1. Prepare participants

1. Open `/login` on the **application origin**, and sign in as an examiner.
2. Open **Academic groups**, create a group, then create student accounts and select that group. The group page also lets you move existing students; an active attempt blocks a group change.
3. Enroll a JPEG reference photograph for each student. Identity checks compare the student against this enrolled reference.
4. Provide students with their credentials, the correct server origin, and the matching client installer from `/landing/` on that origin.

**Ready to continue:** the student exists in the intended environment, has a reference photograph, and belongs to the group that will receive the exam. Development and production have separate accounts.

## 2. Configure the exam

Create an exam with its subject, group, questions, and answer options. Set:

| Setting | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| Scheduled start | Determines when the assigned exam is available; check the displayed timezone. |
| Duration | Core enforces the attempt deadline. Reopening a client does not reset it. |
| Attempt limit | Limits how many attempts the participant can start. |
| Passing score | Determines the academic outcome from answers. |
| Speech, objects, and head-movement policy | Determines which observations are relevant to this exam. |

The attempt captures the exam definition and policy at start. Do not expect later edits to rewrite an already running attempt.

**Ready to continue:** the student can see the assigned exam at the intended time, and the policy matches the instructions given to participants.

## 3. Prepare the student and start

1. Launch the native client and select the application origin. Use HTTPS for production.
2. Sign in, review the recording/privacy information, and prepare the camera and microphone.
3. Close prohibited applications and resolve environment checks. A DEV override is a local development tool, not a production admission procedure.
4. The client lists upcoming and currently available exams and refreshes every five seconds. Check the displayed start/end time (in the device timezone), select an available exam, and complete the identity and timed pose challenge. Upcoming exams are visible but cannot be started early.
5. Begin the attempt after admission. Core controls the deadline; the client saves answers and records camera/screen evidence.

**If interrupted:** reopen the client with the same account and origin. A verified resume restores saved answers while retaining the original deadline. Pending evidence can continue uploading under its separate, time-limited delivery authorization (seven days).

## 4. Monitor the attempt

Use the examiner panel to inspect attempt state, connection status, previews, and observations. The panel uses REST polling; it is not a live WebSocket video feed. Camera analysis, audio analysis, and recording delivery are separate statuses.

AI analyzes submitted frames and audio samples. An observation is a prompt to inspect context, not an automatic integrity verdict. Speech activity does not identify a speaker, and objects outside the camera image cannot be detected.

After submission or expiry, inspect the evidence manifest and available camera/screen recordings. A submitted exam can still have pending evidence deliveries.

## 5. Review and record the outcome

Keep the three outcomes separate:

| Outcome | What to inspect |
|---|---|
| Academic score | Saved answers and calculated score |
| Evidence delivery | Delivered, Partial, Pending, or Unknown coverage; gaps in camera/screen recordings |
| Integrity verdict | Examiner decision based on observations and available evidence |

Confirm or dismiss observations after reviewing context. The review rules require:

- **Accepted:** complete delivered evidence and dismissed observations.
- **Rejected:** a confirmed observation.
- **Inconclusive:** insufficient evidence to reach a supported conclusion.

Delivered describes file/time coverage; it does not guarantee a useful camera image. Late recordings or observations can return an attempt to review without changing its academic score.

Print the report through the browser when needed. The current report is printable HTML, not an immutable signed PDF or LMS export.

**Complete when:** the academic result, evidence state, and examiner verdict are understood and recorded. For exact behavior and testing boundaries, see [Current behavior & validation](/docs/11-current-contract).
